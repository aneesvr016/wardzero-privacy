WILD WEST RIDER - Privacy Policy

Developer: MAStudio · Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: Wild West Rider does not collect, sell or share your personal information. We do not use advertisements, analytics, tracking, player accounts or our own online servers. Your coins, game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

1. Who we are

Wild West Rider is a virtual reality horse riding, wagon driving and hunting game for Meta Quest headsets, developed by MAStudio ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Wild West Rider through the Meta Horizon Store.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Wild West Rider does not require registration, player accounts, personal details or login information.

We do not use advertisements, analytics services, tracking technologies or third-party advertising SDKs.

The following game information is stored locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs:

  • In-game coins and currency balance.
  • Game progress and unlocked content.
  • Player preferences and game settings.

This information is stored locally on your headset and is not transmitted to MAStudio.

3. Information processed by Meta

Wild West Rider runs on the Meta Quest platform and may use Meta Platform SDK functionality to verify app ownership and access platform features.

Meta may process information associated with your Meta account and device when you use the app. MAStudio does not receive or independently store your Meta account credentials or personal account information through this process.

Head and controller tracking data may be processed in real time by the headset to provide the VR experience. We do not record or transmit this tracking data to our own servers.

For more information about how Meta handles information, please review the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. How we use information

Locally stored game data is used only to provide the gameplay experience, save your coins and progress, remember unlocked content and maintain your preferences between sessions.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, marketing or tracking.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Since we do not collect personal information or operate our own backend servers, we have no personal player database to share.

Meta processes platform-related information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your coins, progress, unlocked content and preferences remain stored locally on your headset using Unity PlayerPrefs.

This information generally remains available until it is cleared by the game, the app is uninstalled, or the device's app data is removed. Uninstalling the game may permanently delete locally stored progress.

Because MAStudio does not store player data on its own servers, there is no personal player data for us to delete from a company database.

7. Children's privacy

Wild West Rider is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirements applicable to Meta Quest and the Meta Horizon Store in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data security

Game progress and preferences are stored in the application's local storage on your headset. We do not transmit this locally stored information to our own servers.

Users should be aware that uninstalling the game or clearing its application data may remove saved progress.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the game or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us
