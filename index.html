WARD ZERO - Privacy Policy

Developer: Quantum Edge LLC · Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Ward Zero does not collect, sell or share your personal information. We do not run our own servers, accounts, ads or analytics. Your game settings are stored only on your headset.

1. Who we are

Ward Zero is a virtual reality horror game for Meta Quest headsets, developed by Quantum Edge LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how. It applies to the Ward Zero app on the Meta Horizon Store.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Ward Zero has no sign-up, no account, no chat, no voice recording, no camera or passthrough capture, no location access and no in-game purchases. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

The game stores the following only on your device (it never leaves your headset):

  • Your game options: turn mode, volume, sound, music, vibration, seated/standing mode and comfort vignette.

3. Information processed by Meta

When the game starts, it uses the Meta Platform SDK to check that your Meta account is entitled to (owns) Ward Zero. This check is performed by Meta between your headset and Meta's services using your Meta account; Quantum Edge LLC does not receive or store your account details. Head and hand tracking data is used by the headset in real time to render the game and is not stored or transmitted by us. Meta's handling of your data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

The Unity engine used to build the game may generate anonymous crash or performance diagnostics according to Unity's own policies; we do not use them to identify you. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. How we use information

Settings stored on your device are used only to remember your preferences between play sessions. We do not use any information for advertising, profiling or sale.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any personal information, because we do not collect it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your settings stay on your headset until you change them or uninstall the game. Uninstalling Ward Zero deletes them. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; if you have any request about your data, contact us below and we will respond within 30 days. Data held by Meta can be managed in your Meta account settings.

7. Children

Ward Zero is a horror game intended for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age required by Meta in your region). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Security

The game does not transmit personal data to us. Settings are stored in the app's private storage on your headset.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
